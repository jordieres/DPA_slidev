## Learning objectives

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- <span v-click>Choose the right **channel, code and formality** for each message and stakeholder.</span>
- <span v-click>Deliver **bad news upwards** and obtain a recorded decision.</span>
- <span v-click>Negotiate **resources in a matrix organisation**: interests, BATNA, ZOPA, packaging.</span>
- <span v-click>Recognise **constructive attitudes and blockers** in a meeting, and handle them.</span>
- <span v-click>Use a **standard interprocess communication** routine — (CPD)<sup>n</sup>A — instead of ad-hoc status talk.</span>

## The communication circuit

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- <span v-click>**Sender → message → channel → code → receiver**, always inside a **context**.</span>
- <span v-click>Effective communication: the receiver interprets the message **as the sender intended**.</span>
- <span v-click>**65–80 %** of what we communicate is **non-verbal**. Words and body must match.</span>
- <span v-click>**Formal** channels follow the governance structure; **informal** ones run in parallel. Both exist — which one *counts*?</span>
- <span v-click>**Active** methods (face-to-face, video call, presentation) vs **passive** ones (e-mail, newsletter, intranet, chat).</span>

</div>

::right::

<div style="height:15px;"></div>

## Meetings (L08 §8.5)

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- <span v-click>Phases: need → preparation → invitation → opening → development → **closure**.</span>
- <span v-click>**Constructive attitudes**: facilitator, initiator, problem-solver, inquirer, clarifier, concluder, harmoniser.</span>
- <span v-click>**Blockers**: aggressor, blocker, absent, monopoliser, jumper, devil's advocate.</span>
- <span v-click>**Active listening**: paraphrase, ask open questions, summarise, check agreement.</span>
- <span v-click>Authority ≠ power. The PM's power comes from **credibility, recognition and decision capability** — built on integrity and coherence.</span>

</div>

## Before you sit down

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>**Position** = what I ask for. **Interest** = why I ask for it.</span>
- <span v-click>**BATNA**: what I do if there is no agreement.</span>
- <span v-click>**Reservation point**: the worst deal I still accept.</span>
- <span v-click>**ZOPA**: the zone where both reservation points overlap.</span>
- <span v-click>Know **your priorities** across issues — they are rarely the same as the other side's.</span>

</div>

::right::

<div style="height:15px;"></div>

## At the table

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>**Distributive** (fixed pie) vs **integrative** (create value by trading low-cost issues for high-value ones).</span>
- <span v-click>Negotiate **packages**, not one issue at a time. Why?</span>
- <span v-click>Ask questions; listen for the interest behind the position.</span>
- <span v-click>Separate the **people** from the **problem**.</span>
- <span v-click>Close in writing: who, what, when — and **who must approve** it.</span>

</div>

## A standard, not a chat

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>Villalba-Diez & Ordieres-Meré (2015): PDCA re-read as a **communication standard between process owners**.</span>
- <span v-click>**Check** (commitment, consensus): go to the *gemba*, agree **one KPI**, the receiver explains *why* it matters, measure the current state.</span>
- <span v-click>**Plan → Do → Check**, repeated *n* times between **sender** and **receiver**.</span>
- <span v-click>**Act**: when the KPI plateaus, the improvement becomes the new **standard**.</span>

</div>

::right::

<div style="height:15px;"></div>

## Why it matters to a PM

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>The **sender** owns the process and the (CPD)ⁿA; the **receiver** provides motivation, resources and feedback.</span>
- <span v-click>Shared language → fewer broken channels between subsystems.</span>
- <span v-click>Higher **standardised connectivity** correlates with better operational performance.</span>
- <span v-click>Question: which of your interfaces (S1↔S2, S2↔S5, MF-601…) would benefit from it?</span>

</div>

## Observe, then name the technique

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- <span v-click>**The Martian** — NASA leadership deciding *whether, when and how* to tell the crew and the public. Who decides the message? Which channel? What is withheld, and why?</span>
- <span v-click>**The Godfather** — the meeting with Sollozzo. Positions vs interests, power, BATNA, what each side reveals or hides, and the cost of a "non-negotiable" offer.</span>
- <span v-click>**Apollo 13** — the engineers' room solving the CO₂ filter with only what is on board: a problem-solving meeting with hard constraints.</span>
- <span v-click>**12 Angry Men** — moving a group from a quick vote to consensus through questions and active listening.</span>
- <span v-click>Optional: **Moneyball** (trade negotiation by phone), **Thirteen Days** (formal vs back-channel communication).</span>

</div>

<div v-click class="discussion-panel warning" style="margin-top:.6rem; font-size:.7em;">
For each clip: sender, receiver, channel, formality, non-verbal signals, attitude/blocker, and outcome.
</div>

## The scene

<div class="scene-meta">
<div><b>3</b>players</div><div><b>8′</b>play</div><div><b>Formal</b>meeting</div>
</div>

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- **Roles**: Project Manager · Project Owner (Iberquímica Plant Director) · GITI Technical Coordinator.
- **Public situation**: the cooling envelope in the PIR is inconsistent. The team's design needs ~1.1 MW (three 500 kW cells), which changes CAPEX and forces S2/S5 P&IDs to be redone. Milestone 1 is in two days.
- **Goal of the meeting**: a decision the PO is willing to **sign**.

</div>

::right::

<div style="height:15px;"></div>

## Observers look for

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- <span v-click>Did the PM open with the **decision needed** or with the story?</span>
- <span v-click>Were **options** presented with impact on scope, time, cost and risk?</span>
- <span v-click>How was "**who knew what and when**" handled?</span>
- <span v-click>Verbal and **non-verbal** coherence of each player.</span>
- <span v-click>How was the decision **recorded** (Decision Log / Change Request)?</span>

</div>

## The scene

- <span v-click>Did they **trade across issues** (packages)?</span>
- <span v-click>Was any BATNA mentioned? Was it credible?</span>

## The scene

<div class="scene-meta">
<div><b>6</b>players</div><div><b>10′</b>play</div><div><b>1</b>decision + action list</div>
</div>

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- **Roles**: Chair (PM) · S1 Raw Water lead · S2 Cooling lead · S5 Chemicals lead · S6 Fire & MF-601 lead · Quality / Documentation.
- **Agenda**: freeze the interface table — flows, pressures and tags between S1, S2, S5 and S6.
- Every participant holds **one piece of technical information** that the others need… and a **secret meeting behaviour**.
- The Chair must close with an agreed **interface table and action list** (who, what, when).

## Observers look for

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- <span v-click>Identify each **attitude / blocker** (L08 §8.5.4–8.5.5). Evidence?</span>
- <span v-click>How did the Chair **neutralise** blockers?</span>
- <span v-click>Did all hidden facts **surface**? Which one did not, and why?</span>
- <span v-click>Was there **active listening** (paraphrase, summary)?</span>
- <span v-click>Quality of the **closure**: decision, owners, dates, record.</span>

</div>

## The scene

<div class="scene-meta">
<div><b>2</b>players per pair</div><div><b>2 × 5′</b>rounds</div><div><b>1</b>KPI</div>
</div>

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- **Roles**: Sender — process owner of a subsystem package · Receiver — PM / quality lead.
- **Round A** — *free format*: "tell me how it is going".
- **Round B** — *standard format*: use the (CPD)ⁿA one-page card: Check → Plan → Do → Check with **one KPI**.
- The sender's card holds the facts; the receiver's card holds what the Steering Committee will ask.

</div>

::right::

<div style="height:15px;"></div>

## Observers look for

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- <span v-click>How many **facts** reached the receiver in each round?</span>
- <span v-click>Number of **misunderstandings** or clarifying questions.</span>
- <span v-click>Did the receiver explain **why** the KPI matters?</span>
- <span v-click>Was a **next check date** agreed?</span>
- <span v-click>Which round would you rather receive as PO? Why?</span>

</div>

## Debrief questions

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- <span v-click>Scene 1: Is it acceptable that the PO hears about IL10 for the first time two days before the milestone? What should the Communications Plan have triggered?</span>
- <span v-click>Scene 2: Did anybody reach the integrative solution? What prevented it?</span>
- <span v-click>Scene 3: Which blocker cost the most time? Which constructive attitude saved it?</span>
- <span v-click>Scene 4: What did the standard format add? What did it remove?</span>
- <span v-click>Where did **non-verbal** signals contradict words?</span>

</div>

::right::

## Back to your project

<div class="discussion-list compact">

- <span v-click>Is your **Communications Management Plan** executed, or only written?</span>
- <span v-click>Status reports: weekly as promised? Who reads them?</span>
- <span v-click>Minutes: written **the same day**, with owners and dates?</span>
- <span v-click>How do you escalate an issue to the PO — and how fast?</span>
- <span v-click>**Task**: add one concrete improvement to your CMP and record it in the Decision Log.</span>

</div>
