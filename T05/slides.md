# Escalation and Decisions

<v-clicks>

- What should the PM do if a Risk Owner reports that a trigger exceeded its threshold?
- Who ultimately decides what to do?
- Is there any value in RMS for risks evolving faster than contingency implementation?
- Can the Project Owner decide not to implement a contingency plan?
- Does the PM still have responsibilities?
- Which ones?
- If contingency actions are adopted, what is the management impact?
- Is there an impact on scope baseline?
- On schedule baseline? On cost baseline?
- Should the revision frequency of the Risk Register be fixed or variable? Why?
- Is the budget for risk contingency measures allocated to the Solution Provider?

</v-clicks>
