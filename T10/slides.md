## Situations

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>Do we know what specific needs our technical team has to carry out the project?</span>
- <span v-click>Do we know whether they are ready to meet the project challenges?</span>
- <span v-click>If they are not, what should we do?</span>
- <span v-click>How have we planned to release resources from the project?</span>
- <span v-click>Given what we know about project methodologies, would we prefer a different one for our project? Why?</span>

</div>

## Stakeholder Management

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>How are we managing our key stakeholders?</span>
- <span v-click>Are we doing it well? What strategy are we using?</span>
- <span v-click>Should we improve anything? If so, what?</span>
- <span v-click>Is Risk Management connected to Stakeholder Management? Why?</span>
- <span v-click>When negotiating several issues, would you recommend addressing them one at a time or taking a different approach? Why?</span>

</div>

## Feedback

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>Have we learned anything so far in our project? If so, what have we learned?</span>
- <span v-click>Have we experienced any conflicts in the project? Which ones? How were they managed?</span>
- <span v-click>Do we have negotiation skills? What do we focus on?</span>
- <span v-click>Do we cooperate or compete in our negotiations? Can you give an example?</span>

</div>

## Groups, Teams and Success Factors

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>What is the difference between a group and a team?</span>
- <span v-click>Do groups and teams differ in how efficiently they produce deliverables? If so, how?</span>
- <span v-click>Rank these factors for project success: having a goal, understanding ourselves and the team, communicating, receiving feedback, and knowing the current project phase. Why this order?</span>
- <span v-click>How important are flexibility, motivation, trust and trustworthiness for a successful team? How would you rank them, and why?</span>

</div>

## Trust and Trustworthiness

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>Does trust depend on what you say or on what you do?</span>
- <span v-click>Is trustworthiness more closely connected to motivation or to flexibility? Why?</span>
- <span v-click>For short-term behaviour, which matters more: trust or trustworthiness? Why?</span>
- <span v-click>What is the main difference between trust and confidence?</span>

</div>

## Team Building

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>A new project starts and you are appointed PM. The Solution Provider (SP) is expected to assign 25 people; 20 have already been assigned, along with you. Do you already have a team ready to proceed? Why?</span>
- <span v-click>Have you invested effort in building a team with your Project Core Team (PCT)? Why?</span>
- <span v-click>What benefits could team building bring in this situation?</span>
- <span v-click>Is explicit recognition of achievements beneficial? How should mistakes be handled?</span>

</div>

## Managing Conflicts

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>Is it better for a team to address conflicts or avoid them? Why?</span>
- <span v-click>Can encouraging conflict within the project management team have positive effects? At what points in the project life cycle?</span>
- <span v-click>Two team members disagree about how to proceed because of quality issues in deliverables. How would you handle the conflict? Why?</span>
- <span v-click>Which conflict management styles do you know?</span>

</div>

## Team Challenges and Meeting Preparation

<div class="discussion-list">

- <span v-click>Have you ever encountered people whose behaviour undermines a team?</span>
- <span v-click>How should you deal with people who undermine a team?</span>
- <span v-click>Is transparency the best way to deal with such behaviour?</span>
- <span v-click>Do you prepare for meetings? If so, what do you do?</span>

</div>
